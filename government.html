🤝 Partnership Vision

SM can be positioned as a unified student-learning layer that organizes authorized educational resources, practice, planning and progress into one simple interface. Any government/institutional integration would be subject to formal approval, technical onboarding and applicable policies.

Digital access • exam preparation • structured learning • student productivity • career awareness • resource discovery • measurable learning activity
